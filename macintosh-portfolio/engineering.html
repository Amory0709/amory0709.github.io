How I make engineering decisions.

The domain can change. The method—understand constraints, identify invariants, choose trade-offs, and verify behavior—transfers.

01 · Performance engineering

Find the bottleneck before adding complexity.

Profile rendering, CPU/GPU boundaries, load time, and interaction latency. Optimize the actual user-facing path, then verify correctness as well as speed.

02 · System architecture

Make ownership and failure states explicit.

Design data flow across UI state, workers, backend APIs, persistent storage, and GPU tasks. Treat cancellation, memory, and stale work as architecture problems.

03 · Product design

Start with the job the user needs to finish.

Work backward from interaction, decision points, and acceptance criteria. A small usable workflow is more valuable than a sophisticated but inaccessible capability.

04 · Delivery and leadership

Keep changes small, reviewable, and testable.

My work includes Scrum Master facilitation, cross-team integration, engineering training, and hands-on delivery—not simply implementing assigned tickets.
